How it works: Claude reads the company's own ASX announcements (titles and Investair summaries, excluding share-issue and substantial-holder notices) and records only expectations the company stated itself. Due dates use the end of the stated period, with Australian financial years (FY = July–June) unless the company says otherwise. Statuses: Delivered (announced within the timeframe), Delivered late, Partly delivered, Pending (not yet due), Overdue (due date passed with no delivery announced), Missed or withdrawn. Each assessment is an inference from the announcements and cites its evidence. Check the source announcement before relying on any item.
