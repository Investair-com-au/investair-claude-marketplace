How it works: Claude reads each company's own ASX announcements from Investair's data (excluding share-issue and substantial-holder notices) and records only expectations the company stated itself. Due dates use the end of the stated period, with Australian financial years (July–June) unless the company says otherwise. Overdue means the due date has passed with no delivery announced. Each assessment is an AI inference from the announcements, with its evidence cited; check the source announcement before relying on any item. General information only, not financial advice.
